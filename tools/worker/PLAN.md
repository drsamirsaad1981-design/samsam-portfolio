# Telegram bot — the plan (easiest → hardest)

Private to Samir first (only his chat gets answers); opened to everyone in the last phase.
Runs in the same Cloudflare Worker as AI Analysis (`tools/worker/*` → `node tools/build-worker.mjs` → `tools/dist/samsam-worker.js`).

1. **Answers you** ✅ — webhook `/tg`, `/portfolio /price /market /plan /movers /signals /menu`, buttons that redraw in place, keyboard under the chat, live prices.
2. **The brain** ✅ — plain Arabic / English questions → Claude with 6 live-data tools (coin, portfolio, market + bStocks, plan, exit, price history); remembers the last 10 messages (/new forgets); 30 questions a day; says where its numbers came from (`tools/worker/ai.mjs`).
3. **Alerts in words + "what if"** ✅ — "tell me if ETH < 3000" (price, 24h change, funding, portfolio value; checked every 5 min by the Worker cron, each fires once; /alerts lists them with 🗑); "if BTC hits 150k?", "if I sell half my SOL?" (`tools/worker/watch.mjs`, what_if in ai.mjs).
4. **Record trades in words** ✅ — "bought 0.01 BTC at 95k" → the bot's own summary (amount, average, realized profit, the stablecoin side) → [✅ Confirm] [❌ Cancel]; only Confirm writes to Firestore (one all-or-nothing write, the app's transaction format); 15 min to confirm (`tools/worker/trade.mjs`).
5. **Smart morning + smart evening + radars** ✅ — ☀️ 08:45 Riyadh: 3 things to do today (DCA, buy price, take-profit, signals, allocation, exit); 🌙 21:00: what happened since the morning, what changed, what to watch; 🧲 leverage radar (funding / open interest / long-short, BTC + your 3 biggest, once per 12 h); 🔥 sector radar (CoinGecko sectors ≥ $1B moving ≥ 10%); 📌 a live pinned message (every 15 min); /daily switches each one (`tools/worker/daily.mjs`).
6. **Image cards** ✅ — in the Mini App: 📸 on the value card, on Market, or tap a coin → a 1080×1350 card (portfolio / coin / market) drawn on the phone (canvas — no CPU on the Worker), preview, "hide $" for sharing, 📤 → a photo in the chat via /tg/card (signed, JPEG only).
7. **Telegram Mini App** ✅ — 📊 Report (menu, /report, the pinned message) opens a page inside Telegram served by the Worker (/tg/app): value, today's 3, coins + allocation, plan + DCA + buy prices, exit conditions + TPs, market (Fear & Greed gauge, ETF, sectors), paper, alerts; English / Arabic. Its data (/tg/api) only for Telegram-signed requests from your account (`tools/worker/miniapp.mjs`, `miniapp.html`).
8. **Voice** ✅ (brought forward) — Arabic / English voice notes → text (Workers AI Whisper, `tools/worker/voice.mjs`) → shown as "🎙️ I heard: …" → answered as if typed.
9. **Paper trading** ✅ — /paper: a separate pretend portfolio ($1k / $10k / $100k) at real prices, 0.1% fee, any coin; "paper buy 0.1 BTC" / "تجريبي اشتري SOL بـ 500" done at once (↩️ undo); compared with the same money kept in BTC (`tools/worker/paper.mjs`).
10. **Open to others (by invite)** ✅ — invites (one code: the bot's t.me link or the app's `?invite=` link; one use, 7 days); each person their own space (`who.mjs`); `/app` opens the app at once for an invited member; members link the bot from the app with a code (app-first members join + link in one step); limits per member (5 questions, quick reports off until premium / their own Claude key), `/forget`, 20 messages a minute, ⏸ pause, 🗑 remove; max members 20 / 50 / 100 from the dashboard.
11. **Premium & support** ✅ — ⭐ Premium = a Telegram Stars subscription (350 ⭐ / 30 days, cancel in Telegram; voice, 20 questions, 3 quick reports a day); 💛 one-time Support (50–500 ⭐, Supporter badge); 🎁 gifts of +30 / +365 days; /stats; 🔑 a member's own Claude key (encrypted in KV) (`stars.mjs`, `byok.mjs`).
12. **The owner's dashboard in the app** ✅ — Settings → Admin dashboard (`samsam-admin.js`, Worker `/admin`, owner only): numbers, members (search, open on a tap, every action), invites with both links, the Stars log.
