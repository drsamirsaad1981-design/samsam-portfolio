Privacy and terms

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In plain words: what we keep about you, where, who can see it, how to delete it, and what you agree to when you use the program.

Who we are

  • Cute Portfolio is a personal, non-profit program built and run by Dr. Samir Saad. It is not a company or an exchange, and it gives no financial advice.
  • Contact: Telegram @smsaad2010 or X @DrSamirSaad1, or /feedback inside the bot.

What we keep

  • Your account: the email you sign in with (Google, or a link sent to your email). We hold no password.
  • Your portfolio: your coins, trades, plan, settings and the notes you write yourself.
  • Cash and certificates (optional): if you add them: the currency, the amount and a name you choose, and a certificate's return, date and term. We never ask for an account number, and nothing connects to a bank.
  • Automatic copy: once a week a copy of your portfolio is saved inside your own account, and the last 4 are kept, so you can bring back anything deleted by mistake. Something you delete can stay in these copies until newer ones replace them, and they are all deleted with your account.
  • On Telegram: your chat number, name, username and language, your alerts, and your message settings. The bot remembers the last 10 messages of the chat for 6 hours only, to follow the thread of your question.
  • Read-only keys: your exchange key (Binance or Gate), read-only, and your own Claude key if you add one. Both are kept encrypted with AES-256 and are shown to nobody once saved, the owner included.
  • Usage numbers: how many times the database is read and which features get used. Numbers only, never the contents of your portfolio.

What we do not keep or do

  • We never ask for or keep the seed phrase or private key of any wallet. The public address only.
  • We refuse an exchange key that can trade or withdraw.
  • No ads, no trackers, and no selling or sharing of your data with any marketing party.
  • Voice recordings are not kept: they are turned into text and then dropped.
  • A backup is a file downloaded to your own device, encrypted with a password you choose. We keep no copy of it and do not know the password.

Where the data lives, and who it passes through

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the database of your portfolio.
  • Cloudflare: the bot's server and its Telegram data, and turning voice notes into text.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the program's pages.
  • Telegram: your messages with the bot pass through Telegram, and Premium is paid with Telegram Stars through it.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when you ask the AI or request an analysis, your question and the portfolio numbers needed to answer it are sent to Claude. If you do not use that feature, nothing is sent.
  • Price sources: CoinGecko, Binance, CoinMarketCap and others. Your browser asks them for prices directly, so they see your connection address as any site you visit does. We do not send them your portfolio.
  • Outside links (such as CoinGlass and CoinCodex) open sites with their own policies.

Who can see your data

  • The database rules let each member read and write only their own data.
  • The bot reads your portfolio to answer you, and never shows it to another member.
  • Frankly: the owner runs the servers, so he can technically reach the database. He does not look at any member's portfolio except to fix a problem that member reports.

How to delete your data

  • /forget in the bot deletes everything the bot keeps about you: alerts, chat memory, settings and the link.
  • In the app you delete any coin or trade yourself, and remove your exchange key or Claude key from Settings.
  • To delete your account and all your data for good: in the app, Settings → Delete my account. It deletes your portfolio and its automatic copies, what the bot keeps about you, your membership and your sign-in. It cannot be undone, and coming back needs a new invitation.
  • What stays after deletion: the record of Stars payments, if you paid (the amount, the date and your Telegram chat number), because it is a financial record. With it, if you come back with a new invitation before the premium you paid for has run out, you get it back until it ends.

Terms of use

  • Not financial advice. The signals, reports and numbers are reminders of your own rules and general information. Your decisions, and the responsibility for them, are yours alone.
  • No warranty. The program is provided as it is. Prices and calculations can be late or wrong, so check the numbers that matter at their source before any decision.
  • It does nothing on your behalf. No buying, selling or moving.
  • Entry is by invitation. Membership can be stopped for anyone who misuses the program or tries to harm it or its members.
  • Premium is optional and paid with Telegram Stars; cancel any time in Telegram's settings. Refunds follow Telegram's policy.
  • The program may change or stop. It is a personal program. If it stops, I will tell members beforehand, and you can download your backup at any time.
  • For adults. The program is for people aged 18 and over.
