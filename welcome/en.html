About

What is Cute Portfolio?

Your portfolio in one place

Every coin and trade, your real profit, and an honest comparison: would the same money have done better in Bitcoin, in gold or in silver?

Discipline signals, not tips

Hold, Reinforce or Sell — worked out from your average buy price and your own thresholds, decided on the daily close, not a passing wick.

A Telegram bot in Arabic and English

A morning brief, grouped alerts three times a day, and questions about your portfolio answered by AI. Plus the members' Signals channel.

Features

What's inside

Accumulation plan and DCA reminders

Set a target for each coin and watch your progress.

Exit plan

Take-profit levels you set in advance, with an alert when they are reached. With the signs of a market top: BTC dominance, USDT dominance and ETH/BTC against your own levels.

Market Hub

Coins, tokenized stocks, gold and silver, and the altcoin season.

The Signals channel on Telegram

Cute Portfolio Signals, for members: the market pulse morning and evening, the sector map, leverage and what the biggest traders are doing, with comments under every post.

Crypto zakat calculator

For crypto against the value of 85 g of gold, and for gold (85 g) and silver (595 g) each by its own threshold, on the Hijri day you choose. The bot reminds you a week before, then sends the amounts on the day.

Allocation and targets

See each category's share of your portfolio and set the share you want. When a category drifts from its target, the card says how much to buy or trim.

Fully Arabic, fully English

The app, the bot and the alerts in the one language you choose, with a complete right-to-left Arabic interface. The Signals channel is in Arabic and English, each on its own line.

Getting started

Start in minutes, not days

You will not type your trades in one by one. There are three quick ways to get your portfolio into the app.

1

Import from your exchange

A CSV file from Binance, Gate or another exchange brings in all your trades at once. You see every line before it is added, and trades already recorded are not added twice.

2

Record in words, in the bot

Write "bought 0.01 BTC at 95k" to the bot; it shows what will change, you confirm, and it is saved in the app.

3

Check against your real wallet

Add your wallet's public address, and the app compares its numbers with what is really there and shows the difference.

Safety and privacy

Your money stays with you, and your data is yours

Cute Portfolio only tracks. It holds no money, and it cannot trade or withdraw.

Not an exchange

Your money stays on your exchange and in your wallet. The app reads, calculates and reminds; it moves nothing.

A read-only key

Binance and Gate balances are read with a read-only key, which cannot trade or withdraw.

Public address only

To check a wallet you add its public address. We never ask for a seed phrase or a private key.

Sign in without a password

You sign in with a link sent to your email, and inside Telegram the app opens already signed in.

An encrypted backup

Download a copy of your data as a file encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256).

The bot forgets when you ask

/forget deletes everything the bot keeps about you: alerts, chat memory, settings and the link.

The project is non-profit: no ads, and no selling of data.

Frankly

What the signals do, and what they do not

  • Not financial advice. A signal is a reminder of your own rules, not a promise of profit.
  • Tested on February – November 2026 prices: an altcoin portfolio that followed the signals ended about 6.8% better than one that ignored them.
  • Over the same period, simply holding Bitcoin did about 3.5% better still. We say so because it is true.
  • Market prices update about every 30 minutes and the bot checks your alerts every 5 minutes, so this is not a tool for real-time trading.

Why I built it

I wanted an honest answer to one question

I built Cute Portfolio for myself first. I wanted to know, in numbers: are my crypto decisions better than putting the same money in Bitcoin or gold and leaving it there?

Then I needed something to remind me of my own rules when the market moves, instead of deciding under pressure. That became the app, then the bot, then the channel.

I am opening it to a small number of people because what helped me may help others. I sell nothing, I give no tips, and the project is non-profit.
